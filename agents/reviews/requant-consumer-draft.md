# Scalar Requant consumers — same-author review

Codex-root applies cqis-numerics, gkr-reduction-author, rfc-author and
soundness-auditor. This is not independent audit. Scope: RFC-0004 A1 / Draft A3,
unchanged RFC-0006 A1/A3 and range_8. [Evidence](../../research/cqis-bounds/REQUANT-CONSUMER.md).

- Public items: static closed scalar derivation, exact byte source routing,
  arithmetic producer and complete full-reveal verifier. No physical model root,
  native reduction, private proof, production stacking or RFC-0022 format change.
- Challenges/absorbs: all child contexts/schedules remain. Thirteen original or
  derived roots and seven verified child states precede final sign A3. Zero
  dimensions retain Instance/Final/records; no new label, retry or random inverse.
- Claims/consumers: each product's expected original root derives from actual
  signed magnitudes, previous product outputs, bounded remainder and static
  constants. No supplied source map is accepted. All nested claims remain; the
  three final sign claims have exact consumers even for zero quotient.
- Bindings/controls: independently valid wrong magnitude, mantissa, bias, divisor,
  scaled quotient and remainder proofs reject their source bindings. Wrong output
  sign rejects SignTransfer; two otherwise valid result expressions reject
  ResultJoin. Shift0 and negative half-tie oversized remainders fail the actual
  range terminal. High quotient bytes remain live under a zero quotient cap.
  Both public unit factors per sum and all three inactive final claims have
  separate one-value controls, including multiplication by a zero remainder.
  Research controls earn no native NC-0100/0101/0109 credit.
- Ledger: retain all 19 lookup identities/reductions, four product identities
  and their A1 losses. Final sign adds 3bo/|K|. Full A3 numerator is
  216+29bo+7br+sum(9bj+18gj); exact tests derive every lookup dimension and term.
  Root/equality routing adds no challenge. Binding, FS/H and joint ZK stay open.
- Static faithfulness: every child schema comes from the expected scalar config.
  Actual byte products and unsigned ranges imply |acc|M+h=qD+r as an integer;
  D>0 and r<D make the quotient unique. Signed sources and nonzero quotient-byte
  sign agreement imply A1, including zero and asymmetric target endpoints.
- Padding/masks: original source layouts include every high byte, with fixed zero
  extension and rejection of excess nonzero tails. Actual child ranges force
  padding zero; full-byte result equality checks all positions. No model mask
  or source ownership is inferred from the scalar statement.
- Determinism: fixed role order, derived root ids, contexts and checked shapes.
  Corpus seed2672 checks 10000 scalar configurations with exact integer rounding;
  the complete proof matrix covers both fields/repetitions and four boundary cases.
- Parity: native/GPU unchanged. Exact integer codec/limb routing comparisons and
  frozen proof-state checks are the research evidence; no independent prover or
  full model/private parity claim. Nested field/Merkle engines are shared.
- Costs: parent routing/equality is linear in authenticated byte payloads, plus
  every existing consumer cost. Verification does not multiply/divide signed
  integers. No measured constant, ledger benchmark or extra weight pass.
- Verifier: production no_std unchanged; capacities and schemas derive before
  payloads, shapes and canonical fields remain mandatory. Arithmetic assembly
  helpers do not authorize complete statements; only verify consumes all proofs.
- Glossary/history: append-only Draft/accounting evidence; original task status
  and criteria unchanged. Native/model source routing, stacking and private
  composition remain required; this review does not accept Draft A3.
