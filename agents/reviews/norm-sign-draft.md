# RMSNorm canonical-sign review

Codex-root applies cqis-numerics, gkr-reduction-author, rfc-author and
soundness-auditor. This is a same-author review, not independent audit.
[Evidence and scope](../../research/cqis-bounds/NORM-SIGN.md).

- Public items: Draft RFC-0004 A3 research for RFC-0022 §2.3's explicit
  zero predicates and canonical sign. No Accepted role, cut or profile change.
- Challenges: full expected function/role bytes, six roots and all three
  verified child states precede parent weights, rounds and finals. Root
  reconstruction adds no challenge. No supplied predicate/table authority.
- Claims: one complete xy consumer preserves both signed sources and all
  magnitude/product ranges. Two further ranges supply actual flags and
  predecessors. All nine parent original claims have actual consumers.
- Bindings: fresh false signs reject for every tested zero/sign combination;
  both wrong predicate directions and a changed predecessor reject. A sign
  substitution also rejects its expected root. Composition-preserving finals
  and a final hidden by a zero operand reject their actual original consumers.
- Ledger: total A3 numerator 131a+148; each nonlookup label has 55a+61 draws.
  Six lookup dimensions are a+2 and four are a+3; each identity/reduction
  remains separate. All16 honest proofs passed exact runtime account checks
  across both fields/repetitions and sizes1,5,16,65; the state hash is frozen.
  The full-row proof remains pending.
- Bounds: whole m<=32768, k<=32767 and Boolean n/s are enforced by actual
  consumers. Predicate sides are at most 65536; sign sides at most 2 and 3.
  Expected metadata supplies every bound. No whole wide value aliases in M31.
- Padding: every padded row flag/predecessor and the unused fourth flag plane
  has a zero cap. Focused false-cap proofs reject padded values, a nonboolean
  sign and predecessor overflow at their actual cap relations. No output or
  quotient selector removes the sign relation.
- Determinism: fixed child/plane/row order and complete canonical norm bytes.
  The 10000-case NumPy int64 corpus passed with a frozen hash. The initial
  control run hit an unavailable subtraction helper in the test; using field
  addition and negation repaired the helper. Verifier and reasons are unchanged.
- Parity: native CPU/SIMD/CUDA unchanged. An actual reciprocal-product proof
  composes on the same xy root; a raw-source sign substitution preserving
  xy and product sign rejects the pinned raw root. Full private/model ownership
  and rsqrt membership remain separate obligations.
- Costs: O(N) inherited xy and two range consumers plus predicate/sign A3s
  in c_a/c_lu/c_sc/T_misc. No per-element proof loop, benchmark or weight pass.
- Verifier: native no_std unchanged. Expected parameters and resource limits
  precede payload access; exact keys, canonical fields, roots and finals are
  checked. The external sign root uses the actual verified third flag plane.
- Glossary/history: append-only Draft/accounting evidence. Original task,
  configured output branches, native/private inference and fidelity remain open.
  Required CI integration and local gates are pending.
