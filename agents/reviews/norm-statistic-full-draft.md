# Full-row statistic review

Codex-root applies cqis-numerics, gkr-reduction-author, rfc-author and
soundness-auditor. This is a same-author review, not independent audit.
[Scope and evidence](../../research/cqis-bounds/NORM-STATISTIC-FULL.md).

- Public items: Draft RFC-0004 A3 research for RFC-0022 §§2.1,2.3,5.
  The full domain uses a separate adapter; the old proof encoding is retained.
- Challenges: complete expected norm bytes, five roots and three verified
  child states precede parent A1 instances. Each actual derived S/carry target
  is in the absorbed instance before round challenges. Finals precede the
  next instance. No new label or column-compression challenge is introduced.
- Claims: each of the four original magnitude-byte claims has an independent
  full-hypercube consumer at its A1-emitted point. Signed/range descendants
  retain every original consumer. A square-preserving final negation and a
  changed factor hidden by zero both rejected at their original consumers.
- Bindings: fresh false S, S+p and carry proofs reject the byte sums. Raw,
  magnitude and scalar roots remain bound through complete child consumers.
  Two INT_MIN inputs cannot replace S=2^31 with field-congruent1. Source and
  output/carry one-value controls passed. Actual old-statistic and numerator
  composition passed; a raw sign flip preserving S rejected its pinned root.
- Ledger: A3 numerator43a+62+7(bs+bc), parent A1 separately6a. Five lookup
  identities/reductions remain separate, at dimensions a+2 three times,
  bs+1 and bc+4. Nonlookup counts18a+26+3(bs+bc) ZeroWeight and
  21a+26+3(bs+bc) Sumcheck. Initial honest proofs passed exact receipts.
  Complete matrix, full-row proof and accounting gate remain pending.
- Bounds: RFC-0022 closed statistic derivation uses expected shape/caps only.
  Full-row maximum side1073709311 has twice its value below p. Whole source/S
  ranges, every carry bound and both zero endpoints are actual consumers.
  No observed bound or supplied certificate authorizes a relation.
- Padding: signed padding is zero. Carry words pad to eight with zero caps;
  S bytes extend exactly to eight slots. Both endpoints, padded carry/source
  and high output controls passed. Five dimension-zero A1s retain all tails;
  focused false high S/tail-carry controls are running.
- Determinism: fixed child, byte and column order; canonical function bytes,
  exact integer arithmetic and no retries. The10000-case NumPy corpus passed
  with frozen hash. The first syntax error was fixed before any proof ran.
- Parity: native CPU/SIMD/CUDA unchanged. NumPy dot/convolution and actual
  old/new/numerator root composition are research evidence. These canonical-u32
  roots confer no physical/model/private source ownership.
- Costs: linear producer and views; independent original MLE consumers cost
  O(N log N), plus complete inherited range/hash work in c_a/c_sc/c_lu/T_misc.
  No benchmark, fitted constant, per-row proof loop or added weight pass.
- Verifier: native no_std unchanged. Expected metadata/capacity precedes
  payload access; exact keys/lengths and canonical field tuples are checked.
  Verification derives byte views and targets, never the integer square sum.
- Glossary/history: append-only Draft/accounting evidence; no new analytic
  public-MLE kind, Accepted role, production cut or NC credit. Remaining norm,
  native/model/private inference and fidelity criteria stay open. Required
  CI integration and repository gates are pending.
