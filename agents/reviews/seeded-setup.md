# Q23 seeded setup review (historical v1)

Follow-up: [v2 all-seed review](all-seed-setup.md) supersedes this candidate.

Reviewer: codex-root, also the author; pcs-engineer, rfc-author and soundness-auditor.
Not an independent audit. Decision: publish the candidate and conditional classical
argument; retain RFC-0010 A1/RFC-0012 A7 as Draft and E4-01 blocked.

- Public items/RFC: formal/seeded-setup.md specifies research-only expansion and
  regeneration. No approved registry, production encoder or profile is added.
- Challenges/absorbs: no proof transcript changes. Fixed setup block inputs are
  unambiguous, separate by seed/level/matrix/row/stream/counter and omit top J for
  intentional tower reuse. They are not alternate Fiat-Shamir challenges.
- Claims/consumers: check_setup compares complete regenerated bytes, not distance,
  proximity or codeword validity. Future authenticated consumers remain required.
- Bindings/controls: research tamper checks reject a canonical changed coefficient,
  seed and exponent. No production binding or NC-0600..0605 credit is claimed.
- Ledger: no production line changes. Draft A7 proposes a separate setup event;
  classical queries have a conditional proof, quantum queries do not. The exact
  quantum counterexample prohibits applying that formula to RFC-0002 admission.
- Certificates: fixed integer dimensions/degrees/caps; no dynamic inference bound.
  Exhaustion is coupled to the uncapped ensemble and rejects without fallback.
- Padding/masks: exact supported powers only, no implicit padding or normalization.
- Determinism: fixed byte/loop order; neighbor sets are sorted before serialization.
  Zero coefficients are retained; independent coefficient streams preserve symmetry.
- Parity: independent framing/row expansion, fixed digests, dense all-field map
  parity at n=256 and shared-tower checks pass. No Rust/GPU or maximum-size evidence.
- Cost model: O(n) bounded hash work, explicit graph/raw/regeneration copies. No
  performance constant or weight-pass change; production memory ledger remains due.
- Verifier: production unchanged. Research validates manifests/raw bytes; complete
  production resource policy, query authority and authenticated admission remain open.
- Glossary: source regeneration, setup distribution, distance, proximity and profile
  admission remain separate. BLAKE3 vectors do not establish an oracle assumption.

I tried to break it by changing one canonical source coefficient, and the control
check_setup rejected it with SetupMismatch. Forced cap exhaustion also rejects the
entire setup. The quantum example rejects the unqualified classical security claim.
These checks are research evidence, not production soundness certification.
